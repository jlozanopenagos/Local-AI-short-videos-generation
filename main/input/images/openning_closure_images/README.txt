================================================================================
OPENING & CLOSURE BUMPERS GUIDE
================================================================================

Folder: input/images/openning_closure_images/

PURPOSE:
Used in Part G (_G_video_assembly) to automatically prepend a 2.0-second branded intro
and append a 3.0-second call-to-action outro card to every generated short.

STRUCTURE:
1. normal_videos/ (Used for EXPRESSION, GAME, and ROLEPLAY videos)
   - openning/<language>/openning_<language>.png (e.g., openning_english.png)
   - closure/<language>/closure_<language>.png (e.g., closure_english.png)

2. fun_facts/ (Used for FUN_FACTS videos with dialect localization)
   - openning/<language>/... (e.g., american, british, spain, latin_american)
   - closure/<language>/... (e.g., closure_image.jfif)

SPECIFICATIONS:
- Resolution: 576 x 1024 px (9:16 vertical ratio)
- Format: PNG (.png) or JPEG/JFIF
- Recommendation: Branded channel identity with bold, clean typography
