================================================================================
WATERMARK / BRAND LOGO GUIDE
================================================================================

Folder: input/images/watermark/
Expected File: watermark.png

PURPOSE:
Used in Part G (_G_video_assembly) to brand every final vertical video.
The video assembler automatically renders this image in the lower-right corner
(at 60% opacity with a smooth fade-in) across the entire video runtime.

SPECIFICATIONS:
- Resolution: ~120px to 200px width (transparent background)
- Format: Transparent PNG (.png)
- Recommendation: High-contrast white or light logo icon with no background
